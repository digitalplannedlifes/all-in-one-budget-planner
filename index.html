Built-in Quick-Start Guide
Offline Manual
1. Getting Started in 2 Minutes

Set your Base Currency and Color Theme in Settings. Next, add your real opening balances in Transactions & Accounts, then enter your Planned Income in Budget Planner and tap Auto-Fill 50/30/20.

2. How Account Transfers Avoid Double-Counting

When moving money from Checking to Savings or paying off a Credit Card, always choose Transfer inside Quick Add. Transfers update both wallet balances accurately without inflating monthly Income or Expenses.

3. Bills, Subscriptions & Anti-Double-Count Sync

Add recurring monthly or yearly bills in Bills & Calendar. Clicking the checkmark marks the bill Paid and logs exactly one linked expense. Unchecking it cleanly removes that linked entry.

4. Offline Privacy & Moving to a New Device

This app has zero cloud servers — your financial data stays private inside your browser. Before clearing browser data or switching devices, click Download JSON Backup and restore that file anytime on any device.
